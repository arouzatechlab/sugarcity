ABOUT US

Sugar City Ambulance Services is a trusted emergency medical transportation provider serving KM Doddi (Bharathinagar), Malavalli, Maddur, Mandya, and Mysore with 24/7 ambulance support, ICU ambulance services, ventilator ambulance facilities, oxygen ambulances, and critical care patient transportation.

Our Vision

To redefine emergency healthcare accessibility in rural Karnataka, ensuring that world-class ICU ambulance services are available within minutes to every citizen in KM Doddi and beyond.

Our Mission

We are committed to providing 24/7 emergency medical support with compassion and precision. Our mission is to bridge the gap between patient and hospital through reliable medical transport.

Message from our Founder

"In a medical emergency, every second counts. Our goal is to ensure those seconds are used to save lives."

Founded in 2020 at the heart of KM Doddi (Bharathinagar), Sugar City Ambulance Services was born from a simple realization: the need for high-spec medical transport in our community was critical.

Whether it is a ventilator ambulance for a critical shift or a dead body freezer service during a family's toughest time, we stand as a pillar of reliability.

YAMUNA N S
Founder & Director

Our Journey

Since 2020, we have grown from a single ambulance to a comprehensive medical response fleet. We identified that the corridor between KM Doddi, Malavalli, and Mysore required specialized ICU setup ambulances.

  • ICU Setup

  • Freezer Boxes
